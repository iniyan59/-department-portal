About the Department

The Department of Software Engineering focuses on developing skilled software professionals with strong knowledge of programming, software development, databases, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and modern software engineering practices.

The department provides students with practical learning opportunities through academic projects, laboratory sessions, internships, and industry-oriented training.
